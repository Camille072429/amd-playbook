2. Inbound Calls & Voicemail Protocol
How to handle every missed call

Missed Call Procedure

Step 1:
Check CRM for the missed call. The number is already logged there. Look at who called and when.
Step 2:
Call them back within 5 minutes. If they answer, qualify and book.
Step 3:
No answer? Leave a voicemail with your callback number + website.
Step 4:
Send SMS with amdwinchester.com link. Log everything in CRM.

Voicemail SLA

5-Minute Rule: Listen to every new voicemail within 5 minutes. Call back immediately. If no answer, send text with website link. Customers who leave voicemails are hot leads — don't let them wait.
"Hi [Name], it's [Your Name] from AMD — I just got your voicemail. Call me at (540) 450-5373 or book a time at amdwinchester.com. Talk soon!"

New Leads (contact within 3-5 minutes)

1

2

3
Book the appointment. Two-option close.
4
Send confirmation text + amdwinchester.com link for self-booking.
Rule: Don't sell over the phone. Your job is to get them to the lot. "I want you to see this car in person — photos don't do it justice. Let's set a time."
